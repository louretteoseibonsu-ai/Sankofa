# New Ad Hooks — "Speak Your Parents' Language" Angle

*Drafted 3 July 2026 — to A/B test against the DNA-kit hook*

## Why we're changing the hook

The first real reply from an engaged user said it plainly: *"I'm Ghanaian but grew up in the USA… I already know my DNA. I just want to learn the language my parents speak."* Combined with 55 website visits → 0 raffle entries, the signal is that the **DNA-kit prize is the wrong bait for the actual audience.** Your engaged crowd is diaspora Ghanaians who already know their roots — they want *connection and language*, not ancestry discovery.

So these three hooks lead with **emotion and identity**, not a prize. The raffle becomes a P.S. (or disappears entirely from the primary creative and lives only on the landing page). We test these against the current DNA creative and let cost-per-tester decide.

---

## Hook A — "The language is slipping a generation"

**Primary text (caption):**
> Your parents speak it. Your grandma dreams in it. And somewhere between Accra and here, it started slipping away. 🇬🇭
>
> Sankofa Twi helps you take it back — learn to speak Twi in minutes a day, wherever you grew up.
>
> Free to start. Built for the diaspora. 👇

**On-screen / creative text:** "The language your parents speak. Before it's gone."
**Visual:** older hands + younger hands, or a split of Accra street vs. a Western city; warm, nostalgic, cream palette.
**CTA button:** Learn more → landing page.
**Emotion:** loss + reclaiming. Strongest for 2nd-gen diaspora.

## Hook B — "Say it back to them" (relationship angle)

**Primary text (caption):**
> Imagine calling home and greeting your grandma in her own language. 🥹🇬🇭
>
> "Maakye, Nana." Good morning.
>
> Sankofa Twi teaches you the Twi your family speaks — real phrases, real pronunciation, a few minutes a day. Start free.

**On-screen / creative text:** "Surprise your grandma. Greet her in Twi." → then "Maakye 🇬🇭"
**Visual:** a phone video-call vibe, or subtitle-style Twi phrase appearing with audio.
**CTA button:** Learn more.
**Emotion:** warmth, family pride, a concrete moment they can picture. Great for a short video Reel with real Twi audio (you already have the TTS/audio pipeline).

## Hook C — "You know where you're from. Now speak like it." (identity/pride)

**Primary text (caption):**
> You already know your roots. 🌍 The DNA test told you nothing you didn't feel.
>
> The real flex? Speaking the language. 🇬🇭
>
> Sankofa Twi — learn to actually *speak* Twi, not just claim it. Free to start, built for us.

**On-screen / creative text:** "23andMe told you you're Ghanaian. Can you say hello in Twi?"
**Visual:** bold text-first creative, confident tone, gold/charcoal brand palette.
**CTA button:** Learn more.
**Emotion:** playful challenge + pride. This one *directly reframes* the DNA angle you were leaning on — turns "you already know your DNA" from an objection into the hook. Likely your highest-signal test.

## How to run the test

- **Keep it cheap and comparative.** Run all three new hooks as a small A/B/C against the existing DNA creative, low daily budget, same audience. You already know €27 gets ~1,900 reach, so even €10–15 per hook gives a read on *relative* click-through and cost-per-landing-visit.
- **The metric that matters now is cost-per-tester (installs), not likes.** Views and reach were fine last time; conversion was the failure. Watch website visits → installs, not vanity engagement.
- **Fix the page before spending more.** If 55 visits still → 0 entries, new hooks just buy more people who bounce. Re-test the live funnel end-to-end first (I can do this), and consider whether the raffle sign-up itself is too much friction for someone who just wants to try the app — a plain "Get the app" button may out-convert the raffle flow for this audience.
- **Consider dropping the raffle from the primary CTA.** For diaspora language-learners, "Start learning Twi free" may beat "Win a DNA kit." Let the raffle live quietly on the landing page as a bonus, not the headline.

## Audience targeting refinements (based on the one real data point)

- 2nd-generation diaspora: interests like Ghana, Accra, Ghanaian food/jollof, Afrobeats, Black diaspora, "heritage," specific Ghanaian creators.
- Geo: Ghanaian diaspora hubs — US (NY, DC/Maryland, Atlanta), UK (London), Canada (Toronto). Your account/founder is Atlanta-adjacent timing-wise; the US diaspora is a strong first target.
- Age 22–40, English-speaking, Android (your app is Android-only — you can actually filter to Android device users in Meta targeting to stop wasting spend on iPhone traffic that can't install).

# Word of the Day — Weeks 3–4 (Days 15–28)

*The second fortnight. Same system: feed graphic in `marketing/instagram/wotd/`, Story version in `marketing/instagram/wotd/stories/`, caption below. Light/dark alternates for a balanced grid.*

> ⚠️ **Verify before posting:** run each word through your app's audio/translate to confirm spelling, tone and pronunciation read naturally to a native ear. Pronunciations are approximate English-sound guides. In Twi, **"ky" = "ch"**, **"gy" = "j"**, **"tw" = "chw"**.

---

### Day 15 — AANE · Light · `15_aane_light`
**Pron:** AH-neh · **Meaning:** Yes
> Word of the day: **Aane** — yes. 🇬🇭 The simplest, most-used word there is. Practise: can you say yes in Twi? 👇

### Day 16 — DAABI · Dark · `16_daabi_dark`
**Pron:** DAA-bee · **Meaning:** No
> **Daabi** — no. 🙅🏾 With yesterday's Aane (yes), you can now answer almost anything. Which was new to you?

### Day 17 — WO DIN DE SƐN? · Light · `17_wodin_light`
**Pron:** wo din deh SEN · **Meaning:** What's your name?
> Phrase of the day: **Wo din de sɛn?** — what's your name? 🇬🇭 Reply below: *Yɛfrɛ me ___* (my name is ___) 👇

### Day 18 — MEPA WO KYƐW · Dark · `18_mepawokyew_dark`
**Pron:** meh-pa wo CHOW · **Meaning:** Please / excuse me
> **Mepa wo kyɛw** — please / excuse me. 🙏🏾 ("ky" = "ch".) Manners open every door in Ghana. Save this one.

### Day 19 — KAFRA · Light · `19_kafra_light`
**Pron:** kah-FRAH · **Meaning:** Sorry / forgive me
> **Kafra** — sorry / forgive me. 🕊️ The word that mends things. When did you last need it? 👇

### Day 20 — SUA · Dark · `20_sua_dark`
**Pron:** soo-AH · **Meaning:** To learn / study
> **Sua** — to learn. 📚🇬🇭 *Mesua Twi* = I'm learning Twi. Tag someone learning with you 👇

### Day 21 — KASA · Light · `21_kasa_light`
**Pron:** kah-SAH · **Meaning:** To speak / talk
> **Kasa** — to speak. 🗣️🇬🇭 *Kasa Twi kyerɛ me* = speak Twi to me. Who will you ask to today?

### Day 22 — AKOMA · Dark · `22_akoma_dark`
**Pron:** ah-KO-mah · **Meaning:** Heart
> **Akoma** — heart. ❤️ It's also an Adinkra symbol for patience and love. *Nya akoma* — take heart 🇬🇭

### Day 23 — ASOMDWOE · Light · `23_asomdwoe_light`
**Pron:** ah-som-JWEH · **Meaning:** Peace
> **Asomdwoe** — peace. 🕊️🇬🇭 *Asomdwoe nka wo* — peace be with you. Send it to someone who needs it 👇

### Day 24 — NYANSA · Dark · `24_nyansa_dark`
**Pron:** n-YAN-sah · **Meaning:** Wisdom
> **Nyansa** — wisdom. 🦉🇬🇭 In Akan culture it flows from the elders. Who taught you yours? 👇

### Day 25 — BOA ME · Light · `25_boame_light`
**Pron:** bo-AH meh · **Meaning:** Help me
> **Boa me** — help me. 🙏🏾 Handy anywhere: *Mepa wo kyɛw, boa me* = please, help me. Save this one 🇬🇭

### Day 26 — SIKA · Dark · `26_sika_dark`
**Pron:** SEE-kah · **Meaning:** Money
> **Sika** — money. 💰🇬🇭 The cedi is Ghana's currency. Drop a 🇬🇭 if you're saving for a trip home 👇

### Day 27 — ADAMFOPA · Light · `27_adamfopa_light`
**Pron:** ah-dam-fo-PAH · **Meaning:** A true friend
> **Adamfopa** — a true friend. 🤝🏾 (adamfo + *pa* = "good".) Tag the adamfopa who always shows up 👇

### Day 28 — NYAME ADOM · Dark · `28_nyameadom_dark`
**Pron:** nyah-meh ah-DOM · **Meaning:** By God's grace
> **Nyame adom** — by God's grace. 🙏🏾🇬🇭 The answer to almost everything. That's 28 words — akwaaba to your Twi journey 💛

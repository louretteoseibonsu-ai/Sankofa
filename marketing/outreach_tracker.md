# Sankofa Twi — Community Outreach Tracker

Where to find people who want to learn Twi, what to say, and a weekly quota. **Golden rule: show up as the helpful Twi person, not the ad.** Give value first (answer the question, drop a free tip), mention the app naturally second. Add UTMs so you can see what works: `sankofaapp.io/?utm_source=reddit` etc.

## Weekly quota (≈2–3 hrs)
- 10 helpful comments/replies across Tier 1 (Reddit, YouTube, HelloTalk).
- 1 Quora or blog answer.
- 3 creator/GSA/church DMs.
- Join 1 new group; post value in 2 existing ones.
- Log what drove installs (UTMs / bio-link taps) and double down.

## The one rule
Track **installs and first-lesson completions**, not upvotes. A helpful answer that quietly sends 5 testers beats a viral comment that sends none.

## How to schedule in Meta Business Suite (free)

1. Make sure your IG is a **Business/Creator** account (IG → Settings → Account type).
2. business.facebook.com → **Content → Create post → Instagram**.
3. Upload the day's image from the `to_schedule_aug05-31` folder (named by date).
4. Paste that day's **caption**, then the **hashtags** underneath.
5. Click **Schedule**, set the date and **9:00 AM**, confirm.
6. Repeat for each day — or do a batch in one sitting; they'll post automatically.

# Word of the Day — 2-Week Batch (Light & Dark)

*14 days of Twi WOTD posts. Each has: on-graphic text + a caption. "Variant" = which creative (Light/cream or Dark) suits the mood — but every word works on either. Rotate for a balanced grid.*

> ⚠️ **Verify before posting:** you're a language brand, so run each word through your app's audio/translate to confirm spelling, tone and pronunciation read naturally to a native ear. Pronunciations below are approximate English-sound guides. Note: in Twi, **"ky" sounds like "ch"** and **"tw" like "chw"**.

## Posting tips
- **Cadence:** one per day, same time (morning works for WOTD). Batch-make all 14 graphics in one sitting.
- **Grid rhythm:** alternating Light/Dark keeps your profile grid looking intentional — check it in the Instagram grid preview.
- **Consistency = the whole point:** WOTD only works if it's *daily and reliable*. Schedule them so a busy day doesn't break the streak.
- **Soft CTA:** every few posts, end with "Learn more in the app — link in bio" rather than every single day (keeps it feeling generous, not salesy).
- **Engagement:** the "reply in the comments / tag someone / drop your favourite" prompts are what feed the algorithm — keep them in.
- **Day 14 (Sankɔfa)** is your brand story — a good one to boost or pin.

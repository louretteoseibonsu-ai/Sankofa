## Full description (≤ 4000 chars — Play & Apple)

**Akwaaba! Learn Twi (Akan) the way it actually lives.**

Sankofa Twi makes learning Ghana's Akan/Twi language joyful, modern, and rooted
in culture. Whether you're reconnecting with your heritage, planning a trip to
Ghana, or just love languages — this is your daily companion.

**Why learners love it:**

• **Bite-sized lessons** — greetings, numbers, family, food and more, with audio
so you learn how words really sound.

• **Sankofa Lens — point, learn, speak.** Aim your camera at anything — jollof, a
trotro, your shoe — and instantly hear its Twi name. Show and say it out loud to
communicate in real life. Perfect for travelers.

• **AI Translate with native Twi audio** — type English or Twi and hear it spoken.

• **Adinkra symbols** — explore the meaning behind Ghana's iconic visual wisdom.

• **Stay motivated** — daily streaks, quests, leagues, and a kente cloth that
grows as you practice.

• **Your Akan day name** — discover your *kra din* (soul name) from your birthday.

• **Modern, respectful, real** — slang tips and culture notes so you sound human,
not like a textbook.

**Free to start. Premium unlocks everything** — all lessons, all Adinkra symbols,
AI Translate, the full Lens experience, and the legendary Ananse avatar, with a
7-day free trial.

Sankofa means *"go back and fetch it."* Come reclaim the language. Yɛfrɛ wo!

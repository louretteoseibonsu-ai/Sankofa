# Sankofa Twi — Beta Tester Feedback Form

Copy these into a free **Google Form** (form title + questions below). Keep it
short — a 2-minute form gets answered; a 10-minute one doesn't. Pin the link in
your testers' group chat and ask them to fill it after a few days of use.

**Form title:** Sankofa Twi — Beta Feedback
**Form description:** Akwaaba, and medaase for testing! This takes ~2 minutes and
genuinely shapes what we fix next. Be honest — blunt is helpful.

---

### Questions

1. **How often have you opened the app so far?** *(Multiple choice)*
   - Just once · A few times · Most days

2. **What did you mostly do in the app?** *(Checkboxes)*
   - Lessons / quizzes · Adinkra symbols · AI Translate · Sankofa Lens (camera) ·
     Streaks / Progress · Just looked around

3. **Did anything crash, freeze, or look broken?** *(Paragraph)*
   - *If yes, please say what you were doing and add a screenshot if you can.*

4. **Was anything confusing or harder than it should be?** *(Paragraph)*

5. **What's the ONE thing you'd fix or add first?** *(Paragraph)*

6. **What did you enjoy most?** *(Paragraph)*

7. **How likely are you to keep using Sankofa Twi?** *(Linear scale 1–5)*
   - 1 = not likely · 5 = very likely

8. **Would you recommend it to a friend learning Twi?** *(Multiple choice)*
   - Yes · Maybe · No

9. **Your phone + Android version** *(Short answer)*
   - *e.g. "Samsung S23, Android 14" — helps us reproduce bugs.*

10. **Anything else? (optional)** *(Paragraph)*

11. **Want updates / to stay a tester? Drop your email (optional)** *(Short answer)*

---

### How to read the results (weekly, 15 min)
- **Sort by Q7 (1–5).** Talk to anyone who scored 1–2 — they tell you what's
  broken; anyone who scored 5 — they tell you what's working.
- **Group Q3/Q4/Q5** into three buckets: **Bugs**, **Confusing**, **Requests**.
- **Fix order:** Bugs first → the most *repeated* confusion → then requests.
- Ignore one-off opinions early; act on patterns (3+ people saying the same thing).
